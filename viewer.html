Welcome

This is a live PDF of the paper: it rebuilds by itself a few seconds after anyone changes its LaTeX source.

Comment

Pick who you are in the toolbar (💬 Comment as…), then select text in the PDF and press C or click the 💬 button that appears beside it.Select text in the PDF, then press C or click the 💬 button that appears beside it. Your note goes into the source and, once the PDF rebuilds, shows in the margin with your initials. Hover over a note to reply ↩, resolve ✓, or edit your own ✎.

Edit

✎ Source (or E) opens the LaTeX beside the PDF, and double-clicking the PDF jumps to its source line. Changes save by themselves and the PDF rebuilds; errors appear in red and link to their line.

Find your way

☰ opens the sidebar: the outline, the pages, the notes, checks, and the figures and tables. Search with Cmd/Ctrl F. Notes: off shows the paper as it would be submitted, with the notes as bubbles in the margin; Notes: on puts them back in the text. ? lists the keyboard shortcuts. The download button (in the toolbar, or Download under ⋯) saves the paper with any notes in it, or without them, as it would be submitted.

To read the notes away from here, PDF with notes in the margin saves the pages as submitted with each note beside its spot.

Where changes go

Your edits and notes are saved to a shared copy of the paper; merges them into the main version.

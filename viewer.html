Welcome

This is a live PDF workspace for the paper: it rebuilds by itself a few seconds after anyone changes its LaTeX source.

Comment

Pick who you are in the PDF bar, then select text in the PDF and press C or click the button that appears beside it.Select text in the PDF, then press C or click the button that appears beside it. Your note goes into the source and, once the PDF rebuilds, shows in the margin with your initials. Hover over a note to reply ↩, resolve ✓, or edit your own ✎.

Edit

Switch to Split or Source (or press E) to edit the LaTeX beside the PDF; double-clicking the PDF jumps straight to its source line. Changes save automatically and the PDF rebuilds; errors and warnings appear inline and in the Logs drawer.

Find your way

Open the sidebar for Files, Outline, Pages, Notes, Checks, Figures, History, and References, or press ⌘K to jump anywhere. Notes: off shows the submission PDF with notes in the margin; Notes: on puts them back in the text. Export saves the paper with or without notes, with margin notes, or as PDF comments.

To read the notes away from here, PDF with notes in the margin saves the pages as submitted with each note beside its spot.

Where changes go

Your edits and notes are saved to a shared copy of the paper; merges them into the main version.
